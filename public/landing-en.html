How it works

Four steps, from signup to session.

1

Add the participant, pick the type (consulting, mentoring, meeting…) and a starter script.

2

Click Present and project only the participant window — no menus, no names in the tab.

3

Type the topics. Each line becomes a live balloon on the participant’s screen.

4

At the end, export the minutes as Markdown, OPML or FreeMind — or the full backup as ZIP.

For consultants, therapists and teams

Each session has a type — and each type has its starter script. One participant can be in mentoring and consulting: the history follows.

Consultants & Mentors

Live visual diagnosis built with the participant — scripts per session type, history per person, and minutes ready as Markdown, OPML or FreeMind at the end.

Therapists

Run the session from the map, keep private notes per person, and track progress with per-participant history.

Teams

Daily standups, brainstorms and 1:1s with per-participant history — and topical minutes ready to go.

For any meeting — remote or in person

At heart, it’s simple: one person takes quick notes while everyone else follows the reasoning — on the call, in the room with the TV, or on an event stage, with nothing to install.

Meetings with participants

Share window B instead of the whole screen: the participant sees a clean map, you keep the rest private.

Classes and mentoring

Write the outline as you speak; viewers follow the thread without seeing your material.

Brainstorms and daily meetings

One person types, everyone watches ideas connect — and topical minutes are ready to go.

In person and events

Project the participant window on the room TV or on stage: you take notes on the laptop, everyone follows the reasoning.

Details that matter

Full list in FEATURES.md.

Radical privacy

Private notes per participant, invisible on the shared screen. Names never appear in participant windows. Nothing leaves the browser unless you send it.

Your data, forever

Everything in plain text that opens anywhere — store it wherever you want, leave whenever you want. Nothing stays locked in here.

Total export

Markdown, OPML, FreeMind, JSON, PNG, SVG and ZIPs — backup with restore included.

Encrypted cloud backup

Puter cloud, encrypted in the browser (AES-256-GCM) — off by default, you turn it on whenever you want; the password stays in memory only and locks itself after 15 idle minutes.

Keyboard and mobile

Enter new topic, Tab indents on desktop and Outdent/Indent buttons on mobile, undo/redo; on mobile, topics and map alternate fullscreen.

SessionMap is open source — github.com/calionauta/sessionmap. Your data stays in your browser; the cloud only joins if you turn it on — encrypted.
